How to claim

1

Mint a take

First mint per wallet is free (gas only), then 0.002 BNB each. Binance Agent ID required.

2

Upgrade the level

Raise your take's level, 0.002 BNB per level. The higher the level, the bigger the pool being split.

3

Claim $CURSOR

Right after you hold a take. Fee 0.00021 BNB per take, your level's pool split evenly among claimers.
