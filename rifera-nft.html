08

Whitepaper

The short paper. What First Take is, how the numbers work, what never changes.

1 · The collection

Rifera — First Take is a fixed collection of 7,620 pixel cursors on BNB Chain, numbered 0001 to 7620. One owner, one number, numbered forever. There is no 7,621 — supply never grows.

2 · The artwork

Every image is generated from its take number by one formula — the same formula on this page and at mint, so the take you pick is the take you get. No image files, no hosting to lose. Every take is one whole, clean arrow cursor; only its color, background, variant, and REC signal differ per number.

3 · How mint works

Mint is gated by a post: post first, then mint — no shortcut. Minting runs through your own agent with a short prompt; this page never connects a wallet. A Binance Agent ID in the wallet is required and checked automatically.

First mint per walletFREE · gas only
Each extra mint0.002 BNB
RequirementBinance Agent ID + post first
ContractNot announced yet

4 · Upgrades

After mint, a take can be upgraded for looks — Level 1 (as minted) up to Level 5 · Genesis Max, at 0.002 BNB per level. Upgrade never changes the number, the owner, or the supply, and there is no Level 6.

5 · Token claim

At token launch, owners claim directly — no snapshot; owner and level are read live at claim time, for a fee of 0.00021 BNB per claim. The pool splits by the take's level:

Level 1 · Base (free)5%
Level 2 · Glow10%
Level 3 · REC Live20%
Level 4 · Prism30%
Level 5 · Genesis Max35%

Each level's pool splits across the takes claiming at that level. Upgrade before you claim — a claimed take is done. Token name, supply, and launch date are not announced yet; announcements land here and on @FirstTake_xyz.

6 · What never changes

Supply stays 7,620. Your number stays yours. The arrow stays one clean arrow. Anything announced anywhere else — any other contract address, any other account — is not official.
